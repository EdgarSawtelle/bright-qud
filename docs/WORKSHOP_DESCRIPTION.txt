[h1]Bright Qud[/h1]
A bright, bold visual overhaul and quality-of-life mod, made on and for the Steam Deck (works on PC too).
Bold outlines, saturated colours, readability first. [b]Gameplay is unchanged[/b]: no stats, creatures or items are edited and nothing is stored in your save, so you can turn it off at any time. Every helper has its own switch in Options → Mods.

[h2]New look[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every creature, character, item, piece of furniture and terrain tile the game uses is replaced: about 3,900 tiles, world map included
[*]Characters and monsters in a "weird retro sci-fi" style; 3/4-view walls, water, plants and furniture
[*]NEW: your character wears what you wear: armour, helmet, mask, cloak, gloves, boots and weapons each show in their own colours and material; 40+ special masks and headgear drawn as themselves; two pistols in two hands, two-handed weapons held with both; slime, blood or frost recolour you
[*]Mutations shape the body: shell behind, extra legs in front, raised second arms, horns above any hat; bold chitin colours per character; wings stay folded unless you fly
[*]NEW: human NPCs (farmers, Issachari, Mechanimists, templars...) show the gear they carry, in their own faction colours
[*]Seed-generated colours are kept; tiles from other mods get an automatic matching restyle
[*]Gentle idle animation (creature and tile animation have separate switches)
[*]Zones with their own look: lavender crystal Moon Stair with a twinkling hexagon floor, lush mossy Rainbow Wood with tiny glowing mushrooms, Joppa's salt-spangled earth, Red Rock, Rust Wells, Kyakukya's cage-fungus houses, Grit Gate, Bey Lah's petal huts, flower fields, jungles, hills and mountains, the wind-rippled salt desert; calm, finely textured ground; small amounts of liquid drawn as thin trickles; warm and coloured light at night and in caves
[*]Redrawn frogs (svardym), reptiles and Putus Templars
[/list]

[h2]Easier to read the map[/h2]
[list]
[*]Red / green rings under enemies and companions, health bars over hurt creatures
[*]Ring under you that pulses red at low health
[*]Items on the ground sparkle; gold corner on stairs and filled containers (F6 or the controls reminder turns them on/off)
[*]Speech bubble on merchants and named characters (fades once you've talked)
[*]Remembered or dark areas dimmer but still in colour; coloured ground instead of black, with its own palette in the salt desert, Palladium Reef, Rainbow Wood and Moon Stair
[*]Flames on burning things, no black squares behind gas, explosions or thrown items
[*]World map: calmer colours; regions too dangerous for your level tinted orange / red, and looking at a region shows its level
[/list]

[h2]Easier to read the menus[/h2]
[list]
[*]Clearer fonts: Clear mono or Readable (Atkinson Hyperlegible)
[*]Interface colours: Qud teal, Tan parchment (light), Amber, Rust, Violet, Slate
[*]Sprite sets: in Options > Mods, creatures, plants, items, furniture, walls and liquids can each go back to the game's own sprites (creatures keep the outline). Visible mutations on characters have their own switch.
[*]Key stats at the top of look / hover descriptions (monster level, HP, AV/DV, attacks, mutations; item damage, armour, resistances)
[*]Mutations and fight skills explained in one line each when you look at a creature (dangerous ones in red), plus its status (on fire, stunned...)
[*]Quest helper: list your quests nearest first (LT + left stick click / F4), follow one and a banner shows the next step and direction
[*]Item comparison with what you wear, in green/red, in the inventory, when picking up and at merchants
[*]Ability bar shows more abilities at once and explains the one you point at or select
[*]Controls reminder (LT + View / F3) and an in-game legend of every marker
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Visual and UI patches only. New tile pictures are spread over several frames, so big fights don't hitch. Content mods work (their creatures and items get the automatic restyle). With other tile packs, whichever loads last wins per tile.
