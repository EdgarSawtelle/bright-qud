# Bright Qud – in-game test checklist

For each item, take a screenshot (STEAM + R1) when something looks off and note the number.
✅ = already confirmed in earlier tests.

## A. Everyday look
1. ✅ Joppa outdoors: ground color, grass, paths, watervine, ponds.
2. ✅ Inside a hut: warm floor, walls with front faces, furniture.
3. ✅ Areas you walked away from look dimmed.
4. Reeds (brinestalk) and watervine now look lighter and clearly walkable.
5. Night or dusk in Joppa: does everything still read clearly?

## B. Animation (new in 0.4.0)
6. ✅ No more vanishing creatures (0.6.x). Creatures gently bob about once per second, all on the same beat.
7. Bats, giant dragonflies and strip flies flap their wings.
8. Your Gunwing character's wings flap.
9. Nothing bobs in areas you can't currently see.
10. Moving around stays smooth while many creatures are on screen.

## C. Combat & effects
11. Fight snapjaws (outside Joppa or in Red Rock): weapons and colors look right.
12. A creature taking damage: hit flash, floating numbers.
13. Death: corpse and blood splatter.
14. Throw something (a rock or dagger): the flying item looks right.
15. Shoot your guns: projectiles look normal.
16. An explosion (grenade): check for small **black squares** around the blast.
17. Fire: a burning creature or ground, and torches.
18. Gas clouds (if you meet any).
19. Status effects: frozen, confused, poisoned (colors change on the creature).

## D. Places
20. Red Rock / any cave: rock walls (3/4 view), stone floor, cave creatures.
21. Salt dunes: salt walls, dune ground.
22. Rust Wells or ruins: rusted metal walls.
23. **World map** (the overview when you leave a zone): anything strange there?
24. A new village (different faction): villagers keep their colors and look like their faction.

## E. Liquids
25. ✅ Swimming / wading in a pond (water under the creature).
26. Creatures standing in puddles: normal ground around the puddle (fixed in 0.3.1).
27. Other liquids if you meet them: blood, slime, oil, acid.

## F. Menus
28. ✅ Status-bar portrait (top left).
29. Inventory, look/examine screen, trade screen, character sheet: item and creature icons.

## G. Mutations (needs a mutated character or NPC)
30. Horns, extra arms, wings, carapace, quills, stinger, beak, claws, thick fur, two heads,
    extra legs, green (photosynthetic) or pale (albino) skin. Works on human-shaped characters
    drawn by the mod (villagers, templars, cannibals...) and on you.

## I. Map helpers (0.6-0.7, each can be turned off in Options → Mods)
33. Red ring under enemies, green under companions; health bar over hurt creatures.
34. Items on the ground twinkle.
35. Warm ring under your character; red pulsing ring at 25% HP or less.
36. Speech bubble over merchants and named characters (Mehmet, Argyve, traders).
37. Gold corner on stairs and on chests that still contain items.
38. Caves lit only by your torch: remembered walls and floor are easy to tell apart.

## J. Effects & regions (0.9-0.10)
39. Something burning: normal ground, flames flickering around it (no yellow square).
40. Smoke / gas clouds: soft grey puffs, creatures still readable.
41. Grenade explosion: game animation plays, no black squares left behind.
42. Thrown weapons / ability blips: no black square behind the flying tile.
43. Jungle, hills, ruins, flower fields: goatfolk, frogs, crows, turrets, mangroves, mushrooms.
44. Ruins / tombs / wooden huts / crypts: walls in the new materials, 3/4 view with dark base.

## H. Stability
31. ✅ Startup time OK.
32. Save, quit, load: everything still looks the same.

## K. Readability & new characters (0.13-0.14, each can be turned off in Options → Mods)
45. Look at a monster, a plant and an item: short coloured summary above the description.
46. Text font: Options → Mods → "text font". Clear mono (default) / Readable / Game default; menus,
    message log and popups change; columns in inventory and character sheet still line up.
47. Ability bar: "More" / "Most" show more abilities per page, names still readable.
48. Point at an ability (touch / trackpad) or cycle with the controller: a panel above the bar says
    what it does; it disappears after a few seconds.
49. New B3 characters: snapjaws (hyena-folk), Joppa villagers, animals, robots, plants with maws.
50. Mutations on a B3 character: horns, wings, extra arms... sit in the right place.
